### [1] [A tidal disruption event in a quasar at redshift 7.19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30441)
*S. Fujimoto,Q. Fei,G. B. Brammer,K. Inayoshi,V. Kokorev,L. C. Ho,R. Li,F. Walter,V. Bromm,L. Colina,P. Dayal,S. L. Finkelstein,M. Ginolfi,Z. Liu,G. C. K. Leung,G. E. Magdis,J. Matthee,R. P. Naidu,P. Meyer,F. Rizzo,A. Robbins,R. Schneider,C. L. Steinhardt,S. Toft,A. Trinca,F. Valentino,R. Valiante,M. Vestergaard,M. Xiao*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: 在红移z=7.19的红类星体GNz7q中发现一个长寿命核暂现源，多波段演化与恒星潮汐瓦解事件（TDE）一致，是迄今最剧烈的TDE之一。

Motivation: 研究早期宇宙（宇宙诞生后约7亿年）中黑洞的间歇性吸积及其尘埃核环境，检验低黑洞质量与致密恒星形成核是否使高红移类星体中TDE发生效率最高。

Method: 利用HST、Spitzer和JWST二十余年的成像数据识别暂现源，分析其静止系紫外光变与1–5μm波长依赖演化；构建结合热连续谱与t^{-5/3}衰减的全波段模型拟合光变曲线；结合独立JWST/NIRSpec光谱中的宽巴耳末线成分及JWST/MIRI中红外测光进行验证。

Result: 源在约2年静止系时间内紫外衰减Δm≈1.1 mag，变幅超过SDSS类星体变率分布99.9百分位，z≳5样本中未见；随机类星体变率复现概率≲10^{-5}；峰值热光度约3×10^{45} erg/s，辐射能量约1.5×10^{53} erg，对应数倍太阳质量恒星被瓦解；红移且极宽的巴耳末线与非维里化宽线区一致；MIRI显示来自约1500 K尘埃、亚秒差距半径的延迟中红外辐射，符合耀发尘埃回响。

Conclusion: 该事件最可能为数倍太阳质量恒星的潮汐瓦解，是最剧烈的TDE之一，提供了宇宙诞生7亿年后黑洞间歇性吸积及尘埃核环境的时间域视角；未来Roman、Euclid近红外巡天与LSST有望大量发现此类暂现源。

Abstract: We report a long-lived nuclear transient in GNz7q, a red quasar at $z=7.19$ powered by a $\sim3\times10^{7}\,M_\odot$ black hole and hosted by a compact, dusty starburst galaxy. The transient was identified in more than two decades of imaging with HST, Spitzer, and JWST. After a rapid rise, the source faded smoothly by $Δm\simeq1.1$ mag in the rest-frame ultraviolet over $\sim2$ rest-frame yr, with coherent, wavelength-dependent evolution across 1--5 $μ$m that places GNz7q above the 99.9th percentile of the SDSS quasar variability distribution and is absent at $z\gtrsim5$. The duration and energetics disfavor superluminous supernovae, and stochastic quasar variability reproduces the multi-band evolution with a probability of $\lesssim10^{-5}$. A panchromatic model combining a thermal continuum with a $t^{-5/3}$ decline reproduces the light curves, yielding a peak bolometric luminosity of $\simeq3\times10^{45}$ erg s$^{-1}$ and a radiated energy of $\simeq1.5\times10^{53}$ erg, which imply the tidal disruption of a star of a few solar masses and place the event among the most energetic tidal disruption events (TDEs) known. A redshifted, extremely broad Balmer-line component in independent JWST/NIRSpec spectroscopy is consistent with a transient, non-virialized broad-line region. JWST/MIRI photometry further reveals delayed mid-infrared emission from $\simeq1500$ K dust at a sub-parsec radius, consistent with a dust echo of the flare. The relatively low black-hole mass and dense star-forming nucleus of GNz7q are conditions under which TDEs are expected to be most efficient. These observations provide a time-domain view of episodic black-hole fueling and its dusty nuclear environment 700 million years after the Big Bang. Wide-field surveys with Roman and Euclid in the near-infrared, complemented by LSST at lower redshifts, may uncover such transients in large numbers.
