Method: 使用 Phantom 代码在三维周期盒中模拟 3 μm 与 10 μm 尘埃颗粒在超音速湍流分子云中的运动；一种方法将尘埃与气体作为两种独立组分（两流体），另一种用扩散近似将二者视为单一混合物（一流体）。

Result: 两种方法给出的气体概率分布均符合对数正态分布且与颗粒尺寸无关；尘埃分布不同，低密度区差异明显；尘气比分布差异最大——两者均在均值 0.01 处峰值，但两流体分布更宽，暗示致密纤维中尘埃浓度更高；一流体在纤维区最准确，其分布变窄部分源于为维持终端速度近似所加的停时限制器，人为增强了低密度区的耦合。

Conclusion: 两种方法均可用于分子云尘埃研究，但应根据计算的数值斯托克斯数区间选择合适方法，以避免数值伪影。

Abstract: We model 3 and 10 $μ$m dust grains in supersonic, turbulent molecular cloud conditions to compare two different numerical methods solving dust coupled to gas through a drag term. One method models dust and gas as two separate species (two fluid) and the other the combination of dust and gas as a single mixture (one fluid) using the diffusion approximation. Simulations are performed in a 3D periodic box using the Phantom code. The gas probability distributions are consistent with a lognormal distribution for both methods and grain sizes. The dust distributions are different in the two methods, showing discrepancies particularly in regions of low density. The most significant difference between the two methods is in the dust-to-gas ratio distributions. Both methods peak at the mean dust-to-gas ratio of 0.01, but the two-fluid method has wider distributions than the one-fluid method suggesting excess dust concentration in dense filaments. Filaments are where the one-fluid method results are most accurate. The narrowing of the one-fluid distribution is also caused by the stopping time limiter used to maintain the terminal velocity approximation. This artificially makes the mixture more coupled in low-density regions. Our overall conclusion is that both methods are viable for the study of dust in molecular clouds, but that the correct method should be chosen based on the numerical Stokes number regime of the calculation to avoid numerical artifacts.
