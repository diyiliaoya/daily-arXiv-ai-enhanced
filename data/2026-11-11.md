Method: 在SPHGal星系演化代码中实现新的可爆炸性与爆发能量处方：处方依赖前身星核心质量与金属丰度，考虑SN失败及抛射物部分回落形成黑洞，并采用与三维CCSN模拟校准的爆发-前身星关系，使E_expl在<0.1–2 B之间变化。对孤立低金属丰度星系运行模拟，并改变输入恒星反馈模型进行对比。

Result: 相比传统CCSN模型，黑洞形成数量接近三倍，但成功CCSN减少对星系恒星形成影响很小，因为失败CCSN比例低。黑洞形成虽移出部分物质，但星系重子循环仍由星系外流主导，致密遗迹质量大多存于白矮星。最显著变化来自新的平均E_expl约为传统1B的33%：导致恒星形成增强且爆发性减弱，外流与质量载荷因子降低，星团形成效率提高，恒星形成区化学富集与金属再循环增强。

Conclusion: 结果表明，亚网格CCSN反馈中爆发能量的真实平均值显著低于惯用的1B，这会系统性改变星系外流、重子循环与化学演化。该结果与低分辨率星系模拟中依赖1B标准反馈以匹配观测标度关系的结论形成对比，提示反馈参数校准需与分辨率及爆发能量处方自洽。

Abstract: Core-collapse supernovae (CCSNe) are a critical source of thermal and kinetic energy and chemically enriched material in galaxies. Leveraging recent advances in CCSN modelling, we implement a novel core-mass- and metallicity-dependent prescription for stellar explodability and CCSN explosion energies, $E_\mathrm{expl}$, in the SPHGal galaxy evolution code. It accounts for black hole (BH) formation via SN failures and via partial fallback of ejecta mass, and adopts an explosion-progenitor connection calibrated to three-dimensional CCSN simulations, such that $E_\mathrm{expl}$ varies between $<0.1$-$2\, \mathrm{B}$. We ran simulations of isolated low-metallicity galaxies with variations in the input stellar feedback models. With the new explodability prescription nearly three times more BHs form compared to the canonical CCSN-model, but the reduced number of successful CCSNe has minimal impact on galactic star formation owing to the low fraction of failed CCSNe. While BH formation contributes to removal of matter from the galactic baryon cycle, the latter remains driven by galactic outflows, and most compact remnant mass is stored in white dwarves. The most significant change is instead brought about by the new average-$E_\mathrm{expl}$ that amounts to $\sim33$\% of the $1\,\mathrm{B}=10^{51}\, \mathrm{erg}$ traditionally assumed in sub-resolution CCSN-models. This reduction results in increased and less bursty star formation, reduced outflows and mass-loading factors, increased star cluster formation efficiency, and enhanced chemical enrichment and metal-recycling in star-forming regions. These results stand in contrast with outflow-launching and baryon cycle modelling in lower-resolution galaxy simulations in which canonical (i.e., $1\, \mathrm{B}$) CCSN feedback is typically assumed to produce galaxy models that match observed scaling relations.
