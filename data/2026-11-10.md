### [1] [First detection of the forbidden rotational spectrum of H$_3^+$: JWST/MIRI observations of the shocked gas in HH 168](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10122)
*David A. Neufeld,Joel D. Green,Dan M. Watson,Paule Sonnentrucker*

Main category: astro-ph.GA

TL;DR: JWST/MIRI首次探测到H3+的纯转动（禁戒）谱：在HH 168中探测到26条谱线，最强为16.325 μm的(5,0)->(4,3)线，其空间与速度分布跟随热激波H2而非电离气体。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: H3+是星际离子-分子化学的基石，其振动跃迁已被广泛观测，但仅由离心畸变导致的"禁戒"纯转动谱此前在实验室和天文源中均未被探测到；该谱线有望成为探测激波分子气体电离程度的新探针。

Method: 用JWST/MIRI对Cepheus A West中的Herbig-Haro天体HH 168进行观测，在两个视场之一或两者中5σ以上探测到26条H3+线；构建转动图，并与解析J型与非解析C型激波模型（以H2转动线为约束）进行比较；另检索档案MIRI数据。

Conclusion: H3+禁戒转动谱可被JWST有效观测，并可作为激波分子气体电离状况的新诊断探针。

Its vibrational transitions have long been observed, in absorption along many interstellar sight lines and in emission from the upper atmospheres of the giant planets, but its pure rotational spectrum, which consists only of "forbidden" transitions made possible by centrifugal distortion, had not been observed in the laboratory or in any astronomical source. Twenty-six lines are detected above 5 $σ$ in one or both of two fields, of which the (J,K) = (5,0) -> (4,3) line at 16.325 $μ$m is the strongest. The H$_3^+$ lines share the velocity and spatial distribution of the hot shocked H$_2$, not of the ionized gas. A rotation diagram yields a rotational temperature of ~ 730 K, but the level populations are not thermal and are best explained by subthermal excitation in gas at > 2000 K. We compare the observations with models of shocks propagating into mildly irradiated molecular gas. Constrained by the H$_2$ rotational lines, a dissociative J-type shock predicts H$_3^+$ emission within a factor ~1.5 of that observed, while a nondissociative C-type shock, which fits the H$_2$ lines better, underpredicts it by a factor 5-6, although the excitation of H$_3^+$ by energetic collisions is poorly known. <details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: CH⁺ 通过吸热反应 C⁺ + H₂ → CH⁺ + H 在暖或激波气体中形成，反应需 >0.4 eV，因此可作为分子气体中能量沉积的示踪物。但此前缺乏对其高转动能级及基态产物转动分布的直接观测，也难以检验该原型离子-分子反应的动力学图像。

Method: 用 JWST/MIRI 对 HH 168 两个视场进行光谱观测，探测 CH⁺ 的纯转动谱线 R(17)–R(31)（13.7–21.3 μm，上能级 J = 18–32，对应 0.57–1.65 eV）。利用碰撞激发可忽略的假设——离子进入 v=0 后在其入口能级以下每条 R(J) 线各发一个光子——把线强转换为基态产物的转动分布；并与热 H₂ 做径向速度与空间分布比对、与 ExoMol 预测比较，同时拟合玻尔兹曼分布。

Because C$^+$, which is common in irradiated and shocked gas, reacts with H$_2$ only when the reactants supply > 0.4 eV, CH$^+$ traces energy deposition in molecular gas. The photon intensity falls almost linearly with increasing J, as expected for formation at a similar rate into every level up to J ~ 33; formation in J = 32 requires > 2 eV of reactant energy. The line positions, up to 150 km/s redward of ExoMol predictions, provide the first measurement of the CH$^+$ rotational intervals above J = 16. A Boltzmann distribution at $φT$ ~ 6200 K, where $φ$ is the fraction of the available energy appearing in rotation, fits slightly better, implying reactant energies equivalent to several thousand kelvin. These lines probe the dynamics of a prototypical interstellar ion-molecule reaction.
