TL;DR: 本文介绍了CREPES算法，通过预计算粒子分布来快速评估宇宙线空气簇射的射电辐射，相比微观蒙特卡罗模拟提升了三个数量级以上的速度，同时保持了计算精度。

Motivation: 在超高能天体粒子物理（>10^17 eV）中，利用射电辐射探测空气簇射具有大曝光量的优势，但蒙特卡罗粒子簇射的射电辐射详细计算CPU开销极大，使得统计研究和优化问题难以实现。

Method: 作者分析了广延空气簇射的纵向和横向发展特征，并利用这些特征加速射电辐射计算。具体引入CREPES算法，预先一次性计算粒子分布，然后从这些预计算分布中获取辐射发射，从而避免重复的微观模拟。

Result: 该算法在保持微观计算精度的前提下，计算速度提升了超过三个数量级（约1000倍以上）。

Conclusion: CREPES算法为超高能宇宙线射电探测的大规模统计研究和参数优化提供了可行的高效计算途径。

Abstract: Radio emission from particle air showers is one of the most promising detection techniques to achieve an experiment with a large exposure in ultra-high-energy astroparticle physics. In this regime, above $10^{17}$ eV, the detailed calculation of the radio emission from Monte Carlo particle showers is sufficiently CPU intensive that statistical studies and optimization problems remain largely out of reach. In this article we examine the characteristics of the longitudinal and lateral development of extensive air showers, and exploit them to obtain a fast evaluation of the associated radio emission. We introduce the Coherent Radio Emission from Positrons and Electrons in Showers (CREPES) algorithm, in which the particle distributions are precomputed once and the emission is subsequently obtained from them, yielding a gain in speed of more than three orders of magnitude while preserving the accuracy of the microscopic calculation.
