### [1] [A PhEW Good Simulations: I. Galaxy Properties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21716)
*Lisiyuan Yang,Neal Katz,Evan Scannapieco,Aditi Vijayan*

Motivation: 现代宇宙学模拟普遍引入恒星反馈驱动的星系风，但星系风与环星系介质（CGM）之间的相互作用分辨率不足、难以解析，导致对反馈效果的刻画存在较大不确定性。

However, like found in many previous simulations, the specific star formation rates (sSFRs) of our galaxies are significantly lower than those observed at $z\gtrsim1$ at all stellar masses. <details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RBH-1是迄今跑逸超大质量黑洞最强证据，但其宽约kpc的弓激波和60 kpc线性尾迹的来源物理机制不明：究竟是黑洞引力作用，还是需要额外的反馈能量源，以及该源需要多强、多近期才有效，均缺乏定量研究。

Method: 采用受控流体动力学模拟，分别考察黑洞单独引力作用与"共动风源"两种情形，比较其能否在周围星系际介质（CGM）中维持与RBH-1类似的弓激波；并改变风的强度、关闭时标、CGM温度与密度，拟合所需动量通量与积质量，同时用准线性尾迹约束CGM的一维速度色散。

RBH-1, discovered with HST and confirmed by JWST, provides the strongest evidence yet for a runaway SMBH: a bow shock and linear trail in the circumgalactic medium (CGM) of a $z=0.96$ galaxy. The bow shock is $\sim$kpc wide, the trail is $\sim 60$ kpc long, and the SMBH apparently moves at $\sim 1,000$ km s$^{-1}$. A $2\times10^7\,M_\odot$ SMBH accreting at $1\%$ Eddington could power the wind. Supernova feedback from a star cluster carried by the SMBH is an alternative, although the required mass and momentum fluxes $\gtrsim 60$ Myr after ejection are difficult to reconcile with a standard initial mass function. For a hot ambient CGM at $\sim 10^6$ K, the quasi-linear trail constrains the 1D velocity dispersion to $σ_{\rm 1D}\lesssim 30$ km s$^{-1}$, above which it becomes strongly disturbed. Runaway SMBH trails therefore offer a new probe of turbulence in the diffuse CGM, in regimes where its kinematics are otherwise inaccessible.

Abstract: Combining the data of multiple pulsar timing array (PTA) collaborations increases the sensitivity to nanohertz gravitational waves (GWs) and other signals correlated between pulsars. The International PTA (IPTA) has combined such data, but historically these projects have taken many years. We introduce $\texttt{MetaPulsar}$, a software package that ingests the $\texttt{.par}$ and $\texttt{.tim}$ files of individual data releases, and turns them into single consistent deterministic models for the combined datasets, compatible with both timing packages $\texttt{PINT}$ and $\texttt{Tempo2}$. We validate the method in four ways. First, we show for two pulsars that the linearized timing solution of a combined dataset does not depend on the parameter values in the $\texttt{.par}$. A mild exception is formed by the Shapiro-delay parameters. No combination considered requires a manual refit. Second, for PSR J0613$-$0200 we show that the residuals are explained by the timing model if we take intrinsic red-noise and dispersion-measure processes into account, and that the single-PTA timing posteriors agree with one another and with the combination, except for the parallax. Third, we quantify the consistency of the timing parameters measured by different PTAs with a two-sample tension statistic, for every pulsar that is observed by multiple PTAs. Finally, we carry out a gravitational-wave background search on the direct combination of the NANOGrav 9-yr, EPTA DR1 (v2.2), and PPTA DR2 data releases, the constituents of IPTA Data Release 2 (DR2). The combined dataset is assembled in under two hours, and the recovered amplitude and spectral index of the common process are consistent with the published IPTA DR2 result. With $\texttt{MetaPulsar}$, a joint PTA analysis can be prototyped as soon as the individual collaborations publish their data, instead of after a years-long reprocessing effort.
