### [2] [Simple multi-interacting fluids in SymBoltz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17384)
*David Figueruelo,Herman Sletmoen*

Main category: astro-ph.IM

TL;DR: 作者在 Einstein–Boltzmann 代码 SymBoltz 中实现了一种简单通用的方式，用来指定重子、暗物质和暗能量之间的任意相互作用；借助符号-数值接口和隐式求解器，模型只需约 300 行代码即可定义，并对单相互作用模型与 CLASS 验证，在多数尺度上吻合 0.1%–1%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在标准宇宙学 Boltzmann 代码（如 CLASS、CAMB）中，实现重子、暗物质、暗能量三者之间的相互作用模型通常需要大量手写代码，改动复杂；作者希望提供一种紧凑、易修改、可处理任意复杂相互作用的通用框架。

Method: 在 SymBoltz 中输入协变耦合项即可自动生成完整方程；利用符号-数值接口生成稀疏、解析的 ODE 雅可比矩阵，并用高效隐式求解器积分刚性方程；先以单相互作用模型与修改版 CLASS 对比背景演化以及物质和 CMB 功率谱进行验证。

Result: 单相互作用模型与 CLASS 在多数尺度上吻合 0.1%–1%；完整模型仅约 300 行代码，新增任意相互作用只需三行；三者同时相互作用等复杂模型可轻松实现。

Conclusion: SymBoltz 提供了一种紧凑、通用且易扩展的框架，能高效实现三类组分间任意复杂的相互作用，相比 CLASS、CAMB 实现更简便。

Abstract: We implement a simple and general way of specifying arbitrary interactions between baryons, dark matter and dark energy in the Einstein--Boltzmann code SymBoltz. Due to the symbolic-numeric interface and approximation-free structure of SymBoltz, the model definition is remarkably compact and convenient to modify by specifying only the covariant coupling. The full equations are stiff, but stable and fast to integrate with an efficient implicit solver, particularly with the sparse and analytical ODE Jacobian generated from the symbolic equations. We first validate it using single-interaction models, comparing their background evolution and matter and CMB power spectra with those from modified versions of CLASS, finding $0.1\%$--$1\%$ agreement at most scales. Finally, we show how our framework makes it trivial to implement models with any interactions of any complexity among all three species simultaneously, which requires a more involved implementation in other Einstein--Boltzmann codes like CLASS or CAMB. Our full model is specified in only around 300 lines of code and is easy to modify for any other interaction by just specifying it in three lines.
